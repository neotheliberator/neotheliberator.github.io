Boat Legacy — Support

Connection and operation guidance.

My boat is not found on Android.

Check that Bluetooth and Location are switched on in the Android quick settings. Granting location permission alone does not turn Location on. Also check the Nearby devices permission.

What should I check before connecting?

Turn on the device and enable Bluetooth on your phone. Check the requested permissions and whether another app is already connected to the device.
