Privacy

Privacy notice for nebukad-international.com · Updated: 20 September 2026

Controller

The controller for personal data processed on nebukad-international.com is Nebukad International UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Buchenweg 3, 35684 Dillenburg, Germany, represented by managing directors Benjamin Ferger and Husni Celik. For privacy enquiries, contact info@nebukad-international.com.

Hosting and technical provision

The website and our email mailbox are hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. IONOS processes the data required to provide the website and email services. The website and mailbox are administered, and contact enquiries handled, by the people responsible for these tasks on behalf of Nebukad International.

Website requests and server logs

The server processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the website and maintain security. Hosting logs include the requested file, time, referring page, browser, operating system and device type. IONOS states that visitor data is anonymised on collection and retained for eight weeks, with IP addresses processed in anonymised form. IONOS reports no transfers of these hosting visitor data to countries outside the EU. Personal data processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and our interest in secure, reliable operation. Details: IONOS Webhosting.

IONOS WebAnalytics

The hosting includes IONOS WebAnalytics for statistical evaluation and technical improvement. IONOS states that it uses no cookies and anonymises IP addresses immediately after transmission. Our website includes no additional analytics pixel; hosting statistics are based on server access data. To the extent personal data is processed, the basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and our interest in technically improving the service. Details: IONOS WebAnalytics.

Email and contact form

When you contact us, we process your name, email address, message, any email attachments and associated communication data to handle and answer your enquiry. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where the enquiry concerns a contract with you or pre-contractual steps at your request. Other enquiries, including correspondence with company representatives, are processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our interest in handling correspondence appropriately. The form sends the message through an encrypted connection to the IONOS mail server for delivery to our mailbox. Your email address is used as the reply address. People responsible for handling the enquiry receive access. Contacting us is voluntary. The form requires a name, email address and message; submission is not possible without them. Alternatively, you may contact us by email.

Protection against abusive submissions

To limit automated or excessive submissions, we process a hash derived from the IP address and the time of the attempt. This identifier is pseudonymous, not anonymous. The separate rate-limit file contains no names, email addresses or message content. Entries count towards the limit for one hour and are removed on the next request reaching this check; if no further request occurs, they may remain stored until then. A form field hidden from ordinary visitors also helps identify automated input. The basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and our interest in keeping the service available and preventing spam.

Retention of correspondence

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry. We then delete it unless statutory retention duties or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims require further storage. Statutory retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR for the applicable period; retention for legal claims is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR until the relevant need ceases.

Cookies, local resources and encryption

The website sets no cookies of its own and stores no information in browser local storage or session storage. Fonts and images are served from our webspace. Language selection uses separate German and English pages. We use no Google Analytics, advertising tracking, external font services, embedded maps or social media plugins. Browser connections to the website are encrypted using HTTPS.

Your rights

Subject to the legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability (Articles 15–20 GDPR). Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time for the future without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Please use the contact address above to exercise your rights. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), including the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Right to object

You may object on grounds relating to your particular situation to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We will stop unless we can demonstrate overriding compelling legitimate grounds or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Article 21 GDPR).

Automated decisions and scope

We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects or conduct related profiling. This notice covers the company website.

Separate GitHub preview

Only the separate preview at neotheliberator.github.io/nebukad/ is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes IP addresses, among other data, for security. Form submission is disabled on that preview. See the GitHub privacy statement. The website at nebukad-international.com is hosted by IONOS.
